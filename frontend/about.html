A wardrobe built to last

Alongside our own atelier line, we partner with houses such as Raymond, Louis Philippe, Zara and Calvin Klein — so your favourite labels live in one beautifully edited place.

Client care

Frequently asked

How long does delivery take?
Orders are dispatched within 24 hours and arrive in 3–5 business days across India. Shipping is complimentary on orders above ₹1,999; otherwise a flat ₹99 applies.
What is your returns policy?
Return or exchange any unworn piece with tags within 15 days of delivery. Request a pickup from your account; refunds are processed within 5–7 days of the item reaching us.
How do I find my size?
Each product lists its fit (slim, regular, relaxed). Adult tops run XS–XXL, bottoms are in waist inches (28–38) and kidswear is sized by age. If you are between sizes, size up for a relaxed fit.
Which payment methods do you accept?
UPI, all major credit and debit cards, and cash on delivery.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes — orders can be cancelled from your account until they begin processing.
